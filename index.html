The Problem

Three barriers blocking
rural education in Vietnam

Despite Vietnam's national progress, students in remote mountainous provinces face structural disadvantages that national statistics hide.

5–15km
Daily walk for some students
Geographic Barriers

Children in Đắk Nông & Lào Cai travel mountain roads daily — especially dangerous in the rainy season. Transport difficulty is a top-3 reason for dropout among rural students.

60%+
Mountain CLCs lack books & power
Resource Deficits

Vietnam's Community Learning Centres are mandated by law, but over 60% in mountainous provinces lack materials, electricity, or active management. They exist on paper — not in practice.

1 in 3
Rural schools lack stable internet
Digital Divide

Vietnam's digital education push has accelerated access in cities. Rural Central Highlands and Northern Uplands remain severely underserved — students are cut off from online learning entirely.

Sources: UNICEF Vietnam (2021), MOET CLC Review (2020), GSMA Mobile Connectivity Index (2023)
"For many of these students, a learning hub will be their first experience with a computer, strong internet, and a trained adult helping them use it."
What We Do

Four components.
One integrated solution.

We upgrade existing school buildings — connecting physical infrastructure, digital access, structured learning, and transport into one program at four schools in Đắk Nông Province.

01
🏫
Renovated Learning Hubs

We upgrade existing rooms inside four schools into fully equipped library-digital centers — open during school hours and after school for community use.

4 Schools 25–30 Computers Each Books & Shelving After-School Access
02
📶
Digital Access & Pocket Wi-Fi

Every hub gets fixed broadband or 4G LTE internet with offline backup. Pocket Wi-Fi units distributed directly to students. Tech tutorials provided for first-time users.

Fixed + 4G/LTE Offline Backup Pocket Wi-Fi Tech Tutorials
03
📖
Literacy & Online Tutoring

Room to Read runs structured reading programs at each hub. Students access live and recorded tutoring in Vietnamese, Math, and English — available 24/7 around family schedules.

Room to Read Live & Async 24/7 Access 3 Subjects
04
🚲
Bicycle Program

800 bikes, helmets, and locks distributed across all four schools over three years. A community mechanic network handles ongoing maintenance — keeping students moving and in school.

800 Bicycles Helmets & Locks Community Mechanics ~$250 Per Student

Partnerships

BridgePath leads.
Specialists deliver.

Four expert partners — each contributing what decades of experience only they can bring. One contract. One point of accountability.

Sunflower Mission

Decades of school renovation experience in rural Vietnam. Deep community trust. Works alongside us during the program, then takes over as long-term caretaker of all four school hubs after Year 3.

Infrastructure Renovation Long-Term Steward
Room to Read

The leading school library and literacy organization across Asia — their model is adopted nationally by Vietnam's Ministry of Education. Trains facilitators, runs reading programs, continues independently after Year 3.

Literacy Library Design Facilitator Training

Vietnam's Department of Education and Training. Selects the four schools, provides official approvals, connects hubs to national education frameworks, and shares attendance data for monitoring.
